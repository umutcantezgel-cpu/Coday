# Digital Masterpiece for a Master Craftsman

<a href="https://www.batherm.de" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="inline-block bg-primary text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-full font-bold mb-8 hover:bg-secondary transition-colors">

Batherm has stood for craftsmanship excellence in sanitary, heating, and air conditioning for over 20 years. As a certified master business from the heart of Berlin, it was crucial to make this quality and expertise tangible digitally. The old website could no longer meet the standards – it was time for a digital transformation.

---

## 🔍 The Starting Point

Before we began work, we conducted a comprehensive analysis of the existing digital presence. The results were sobering:

### The Problems in Detail

**1. Outdated Design without Recognition Value**

The existing website looked like it was from another era. Stock photos unrelated to the actual team, generic layouts, and a design that undermined trust rather than building it. In an industry where customers let craftsmen into their homes, visual trust is decisive.

**2. Catastrophic Mobile Performance**

With over 68% mobile traffic in the craft sector, the non-responsive website was a real business problem. Potential customers abandoned the visit before they could even find the phone number.

**3. Invisible in Local Search Results**

Despite excellent reviews on other platforms, Batherm did not appear in local search results. The competition dominated search queries like "Heating installer Berlin" or "Sanitary emergency service".

<ExpandableSection title="Why local visibility is so important" variant="highlight">
  **82% of all local search queries** lead to a contact or visit within 24 hours. For craft businesses, this means: If you don't appear on page 1 of Google, you effectively don't exist for most potential customers. 
  
  Especially critical: In emergencies like a pipe burst, people google "Plumber emergency service [District]" – and call the first result. The difference between 1st and 5th place can mean thousands of Euros in revenue per month.
</ExpandableSection>

---

## Our Strategy

After the analysis phase, we developed a three-part strategy addressing all identified problems:

### 1. Trust-First Design Philosophy

We focused on real images of the team, the workshop, and completed projects. Every element was designed to build trust – from prominent certificate badges to video testimonials from satisfied customers.

### 2. Mobile-First Development

The entire website was conceived from the ground up for mobile devices. Large touch targets, fast load times, and a click-to-call button immediately visible on every screen.

### 3. Local SEO Dominance

A comprehensive package of technical SEO, content strategy, and Google Business Profile optimization to become the digital number 1 in the region.

In an intensive kickoff workshop, we got to know the Batherm team personally. We spoke with the managing director about his vision, interviewed employees about their strengths, and analyzed the successful projects of recent years.

Based on the insights, we developed a visual concept combining tradition and modernity. The color palette – a deep petrol combined with warm accents – was intended to radiate trust and professionalism.

## Key Features in Detail

### Trust-Building Elements

We strategically placed trust-building elements throughout the website:

- **Certificate Badges** in the header (TÜV, Guild Master, etc.)
- **Video Testimonials** from real customers
- **Before/After Galleries** of completed projects
- **Team Page** with personal introductions of all employees
- **Real-time Availability Display** for emergency assignments

### Service-First Architecture

Navigation was completely structured around services:

1. **Heating** – From classic gas heating to heat pumps
2. **Sanitary** – Bathroom renovation, pipeline construction, emergency service
3. **Air Conditioning** – installation and maintenance
4. **Emergency Service** – 24/7 availability for emergencies

### Local SEO Measures

<ExpandableSection title="The Complete Local SEO Checklist" variant="highlight">
  **On-Page Optimization:**
  - ✅ NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone) on all pages
  - ✅ Local keywords in Title Tags and Meta Descriptions
  - ✅ Schema.org LocalBusiness Markup
  - ✅ Embedded Google Maps with correct location
  - ✅ District-specific landing pages
  
  **Off-Page Optimization:**
  - ✅ Google Business Profile fully optimized
  - ✅ Entries in relevant business directories
  - ✅ Active review strategy
  - ✅ Local backlinks from partner businesses
  
  **Technical SEO:**
  - ✅ Mobile-First Indexing compatible
  - ✅ Core Web Vitals in the green
  - ✅ HTTPS with correct SSL certificate
  - ✅ Fast server response times (< 200ms)
</ExpandableSection>

## Key Learnings

What we took away from this project:

1. **Authenticity beats Perfection** – Real photos of the real team perform better than polished stock photos
2. **Mobile is not optional** – In the craft sector, over 70% of traffic comes from smartphones
3. **Local SEO takes patience** – First rankings appeared after 2 weeks, full impact after 3 months
4. **Conversion Optimization from Day 1** – Every page needs a clear call to action
